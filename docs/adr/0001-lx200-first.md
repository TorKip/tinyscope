# The mount speaks LX200 over TCP first

`mountd` exposes the mount through the Meade LX200 text protocol over TCP rather than as a native INDI driver. LX200 is the protocol of my own LX200 10" and of countless other mounts, so implementing it teaches the protocol from the inside, and any client that speaks it (Ekos through INDI's LX200 driver, SkySafari) can drive tinyscope with no driver of our own. ASCOM Alpaca comes later as a second front-end on the same core, for N.I.N.A.

## Consequences

- LX200 has no standard status or error reporting, and its precision modes are a legacy quirk; `mountd` must handle both.
- To check: that INDI's LX200 driver connects over TCP, not only serial.
