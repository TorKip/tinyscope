# Article: plan and material

*Started 2026-10-22. For the local astronomy magazine and the local astronomy
Facebook group (in Norwegian). This file collects what it needs along the
way, so nothing has to be reconstructed.*

## The angle (draft)

"How much telescope do you get for almost nothing, and what does each upgrade
really buy?" A naive rig, measured honestly, then improved one hypothesis at
a time, with the same targets photographed on every rig up to the EQ5 and the
LX200 10".

## Collect as we go

- **Photos of the build**: prints, wiring, the rig on the tripod, the setup
  in the garden. Also the failures.
- **The benchmark previews** (B1–B5 in [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md)), same
  targets, rig by rig.
- **One scoreboard table** per rig: setup time, zero point, limiting
  magnitude, pointing error, tracking drift.
- **Hypotheses that failed**, and why (from `docs/experiments/`): often the
  most interesting part.
- **Costs**, in kroner, per rig.

## Possible timing

A first article after S4: the camera on a tripod (phase 0) vs the naive
tracked tinyscope. Later ones as upgrades and the bigger mounts are added.
